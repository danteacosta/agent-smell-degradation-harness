# Source-based pilot screening

Screening completed on 6 September 2026. The subsequent
[96-call diagnostic](source-diagnostic-results.md) also completed and failed its
frozen gate. No prospective pilot trajectory has been collected. Screening is
instrument preparation, not a result about semantic preservation or H1/H2.

## What was observed

The first screening issued 60 calls: both providers reviewed 24 source/variant
packets and six source-control constructions. Cost: US$0.023043. All 48 paired
reviews were schema-valid. Ten of twelve control reviews were schema-valid;
two reached their output limit and remain recorded as invalid.

Source-control review exposed missing source qualifiers in some condensed
clauses. A pre-diagnostic revision restored subjects, obligation modality,
conditions, syntax examples and separately listed capabilities. The six source
seeds, background contexts, natural sample and evidence-v2 outcome-judge prompt
did not change. These are revised constructions, not six new independent cases.

Only the twelve control reviews were recollected, with a larger output bound.
Eleven returned complete schema-valid responses. One still reached the bound
and is preserved as invalid; its text was not repaired into a valid response.
The additional cost was US$0.008774, for **US$0.031817 across 72 actual calls**.
All charges have verified usage; no reservation is pending. The current review
set contains 59 schema-valid responses out of 60; the historical attempt stays
visible, including its two invalid responses.

## Admission and limits

The operator recorded source/license, revision, independence and manipulation
dispositions for all 24 intents. This is **AI-assisted exploratory admission**,
not human validation or confirmatory admission. All paired reviews identified
the changed variant, but that task exposes both versions and the source. It is
easier than evaluating a generated artifact and does not estimate judge accuracy.

The revised source clauses were checked directly against their source text as a
conjunction, not by demanding that every individual clause repeat the complete
requirement. This resolves objections about splitting lists or changing plural
"functions" to singular "function" in distributed clauses. Likewise, a reviewer
statement that the target is absent from unrelated background context is not
evidence of target leakage.

For the one incomplete control review, admission rests on the explicit source
mapping and the operator's documented check, with the review-format limitation
retained. **There are not two valid independent approvals for that control.**
No model consensus is treated as ground truth. The source guards, compound
deletions, related source families and pre-existing underspecification remain
visible in the private admission record and the public protocol.
