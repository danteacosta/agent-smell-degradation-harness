# Average precision correction and synthetic report scope

The audit reproduced two problems: identical scores could receive AP=1.0 when
positive labels appeared first (rather than prevalence=0.5), and train-family
selection could prefer a constant scorer over a useful scorer. Seven AP paths
computed precision at individual rows instead of complete score thresholds;
two additionally broke score ties with the outcome label itself.

## Correction

All seven paths now share `protocol.metrics.average_precision`. It aggregates
all observations at the same score before calculating precision and the recall
increment. It rejects mismatched lengths, non-binary labels, and non-finite
scores. Public imports remain available. Empty/no-positive AP remains zero as
an explicit descriptive convention; this does not establish discrimination.
The precision simulator retains its no-positive error policy.

The definition follows the official scikit-learn reference:
[average_precision_score](https://scikit-learn.org/1.5/modules/generated/sklearn.metrics.average_precision_score.html)
(accessed 2026-09-21). This is non-interpolated average precision, not trapezoidal
area. No runtime dependency on scikit-learn was introduced.

## Impact and interpretation

Potentially affected derived outputs are synthetic analysis, pre-pilot provenance
AP, group-split H2 diagnostics, confirmatory H2 AP/ablation/bootstrap summaries,
legacy train-family selection, and precision simulations. Results with tied
scores must be recomputed from frozen inputs before further scientific use;
untied valid inputs retain the same mathematical estimator. This note does not
claim that every historical run was affected or silently replace any artifact.
Raw observations, labels, dataset splits, E2E results and frozen private packets
are unchanged. No new provider calls or human annotation were made.

`make analysis` now emits `analysis_scope=synthetic_demonstration_only`,
`confirmatory_eligible=false`, the AP definition version and concrete limitations.
Its legacy H1/H2 field names are retained for readers, but those values come from
synthetic fixtures, injected failures, full traces and oracle-derived labels.
They are not the held-out, human-labelled pre-final estimands of the thesis.

## Verification contract

Known examples cover all permutations of tied outcomes, mixed thresholds,
perfect/reversed rankings, rejected invalid inputs, and a public family-selection
journey. A serialized-report integration test requires non-confirmatory scope.
Historical raw evidence is preserved rather than regenerated by these tests.
