## Read this first

11 improvements, 44 ties and 5 unknowns**. Six improvements belong to three

A passing output after an omission establishes local behavioral success.
It does not identify whether the model used the scaffold, prior knowledge,
another requirement sentence or a guess. The absence of a clause from the
edited paragraph is different from its absence from the complete input.

## What the five papers change in the argument

These are bounded uses of the author manuscripts, re-read on 2026-10-01.
Peer review was not independently verified; Gherkin reports SEET acceptance.
They do not establish H1/H2, client value or priority over all related work.

- [ClarEval, §3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.00187v1): human review of injected ambiguity motivates explicit manipulation review. Here that means checking the named defect, the affected obligation and other changed information before using the pair as a smell contrast. Its synthetic benchmark does not validate our mappings.
- [Ask or Assume, Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.26233v3): the authors distinguish essential removed information from details recoverable using remaining context. This motivates the exposure audit above. Its agent actively obtains new information; it is a conceptual comparator, not automatically our B0. Any adaptation requires a separate information, tool and cost budget review.
- [SWE-RPG, §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09072v1): full-trajectory diagnosis uses intermediate reference information points. Our proposed comparison must ask whether pre-T4 signals add predictive value under matched information boundaries. Information-point coverage overlaps our motivation; merely renaming points as constraints is not novelty. Earliest-loss-stage remains a proposed secondary readout, not retrospectively generated ground truth.
- [SlopCodeBench, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.24755v2): iterative structural erosion is a related but distinct outcome. We retain condition-level loss as the construct rather than adding code verbosity to the H1/H2 labels.
- [Gherkin, §§4.4 and 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01980): disagreement and single-run limitations motivate independent rubric calibration and repeated runs. The paper recommends complementary coverage measures; it does not demonstrate that ordinal alpha or 0.70 is correct here. The synthetic scale-offset test checks policy sensitivity, not annotator reliability. Ordinal semantics and thresholds still require justification before confirmation.

## Decisions for the next meeting

1. What is the intended information boundary? For example, should Favorites plus `favoritedBy` remain a realistic context, or should a separately designed arm measure removal from every information channel? Decide before collecting; retain today's null cases either way.
2. Is the Nextcloud estimand a documentation repair including linked guidance, or one sentence? Current results support only the former. A sentence-only study would need a new freeze and cannot overwrite this one.
3. Can independent reviewers map the six candidate historical smells without seeing E2E outcomes? For example, is “three statuses” ambiguous to them, or conventional shorthand for one of three?
4. Does the corrected Rotate excerpt specify the tested action? If not, qualify the requirement/oracle mapping before spending on more runs.
5. Do the severity anchors justify ordinal distance and the agreement thresholds? A higher alpha in a synthetic example is not a justification.

assembly. No review has been performed or approved by this audit. A reviewer
who has read this report must declare that exposure; their later judgment
cannot be described as outcome-blind.

Use the existing review workflows where their contracts fit. Do not coerce

existing natural-rubric-v1 taxonomy does not cover every incompleteness claim

### Review export boundary correction

The existing exporter previously treated the caller's working directory as
the repository boundary. Running it from elsewhere could accept an output
inside this checkout and reject a legitimate external output. The boundary
now derives from the module's checkout location. Three regression cases
reproduce both unsafe acceptance paths and the false rejection before the fix,
then pass afterward. This protects this checkout's boundary; it is not a
universal detector of other repositories or a security certification.
