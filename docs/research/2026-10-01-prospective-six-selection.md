# Prospective six-case H1 browser selection

The [machine-readable register](../../data/prospective-h1-six-20261001/selection.json) fixes six **candidate identities** before any generation **in this cohort**. It is a selection freeze, not admission, provider qualification or an H1 result. Six distinct open-source projects have pinned source and license bytes; all 12 file hashes were verified. If all six later pass the gates, A/B/C × two models × three repetitions would yield at most **108 new positions**. The realized denominator will include deferred slots and provider/browser unknowns rather than silently replacing them. **Two cases are explicitly replications of exploratory pilots, not first executions.**

The earlier 26-change historical catalog cannot supply six unused eligible cases: its [closure](2026-09-30-natural-e2e-closure.md) records ten executed requirements, fourteen noncomparable records, one unqualified record, and one duplicate treatment. The separate 12-slot prospective pool has already executed eight new obligations and one TodoMVC bridge. **A custody audit after the first publication of this selection found later TodoMVC Clear completed and RealWorld comment Delete pilots that the public admission reports omitted.** Their sealed results are described in the [custody correction](2026-10-01-unreported-browser-pilots-custody.md). Neither has been generated in this cohort. They remain selected only as *outcome-informed replications*. Nextcloud, StrictDoc, Kanboard and Paperless are the four obligations without known prior generation. The analyst has seen previous project- and case-level outcomes, so selection is purposive and not researcher-blind or random; this cohort cannot support a population prevalence claim. With one obligation per project, requirement and project are the same six units here: they cannot supply separate estimates of variation at both levels.

For every slot, the ATDD contract is: given its pinned source and a qualified independent reference, when A, B and C generate the bounded behavior in separate contexts, the same frozen browser journey classifies target compliance, unrelated failures, invalid output and missing observation. A and B retain the obligation; C deletes one obligation and changes nothing else. A valid baseline must satisfy non-target controls, and target-only mutants must fail only the target. The interface must not reveal the omitted answer; two distinct correct implementations, target and non-target mutants, duplicate/missing UI and runtime errors must qualify in the final browser runtime. An independent source-to-oracle review examines exact excerpts and the A/B equivalence before any provider call. Failed admissions remain visible under their original IDs. No substitution or retrospective oracle revision enters the frozen cohort; a change requires a separately versioned successor.

The manipulated family is a **proposed** instance of *Incomplete System Behavior* as defined for use-case descriptions by [Seki et al.](2026-09-30-literature-validated-smell-audit.md), because C suppresses one necessary behavior known from the independently pinned complete source. That literature mapping does not prove a natural upstream smell, and its transfer to product documentation needs independent review. Browser pass/failure is a behavioral readout, not H1's ordinal quality/severity label. Formal H1 requires blinded independent labels and a requirement/project-level analysis; H2 is outside this cohort.
