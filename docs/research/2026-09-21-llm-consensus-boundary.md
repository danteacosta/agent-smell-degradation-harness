# What an automated consensus can establish

## Decision and source

The user authorized completing autonomous research work with an LLM judging
consensus in a separate exploratory study. This changes the operational scope
of the next collection, not the status of previous failed gates or the human
annotation requirements of the confirmatory protocol.

Zheng et al., *Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena*,
NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks, [version 4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685v4),
accessed 2026-09-21, examines position, verbosity and self-enhancement biases
and limitations of judge reasoning. Its human-preference benchmark does not
validate semantic coverage labels for this thesis. The local design therefore
uses isolated judgments, hides variant identities and preserves disagreement.

## Local methodological choices

Three requested model configurations receive the same complete source rubric
and one generated artifact at a time. They do not receive the generation prompt,
condition identity, paired output, prior judges' answers or desired result.
Separate calls provide procedural separation, not independent training data or
independent error mechanisms. All configurations share one provider; the CLI
does not expose immutable response snapshots.

Calibration checks author-constructed examples before study generation. Passing
checks supports bounded instrument use; it does not estimate accuracy on natural
requirements. Calibration failures and invalid outputs remain outcomes. A fixed
unanimity rule preserves unresolved cells; majority agreement is a separately
reported sensitivity result, never a replacement for individual votes.

An explicit quotation proves location in an artifact. Semantic support still
depends on the judge and rubric. A shared hallucination can receive unanimous
votes. A source-level omission in generated criteria is also distinct from a
runtime defect in generated software.

The six-category SRS-163 rubric does not resolve whether every field is required
for each command. It measures presence of source obligations in criteria at the
collective level. The uncertainty field cannot satisfy a criterion. Results from
one source intent remain descriptive, regardless of repetitions or vote count.

## Consequences for reporting

All outputs use `label_source: llm_panel`, `human_approvals: 0` and
`confirmatory_eligible: false`. No existing review form is filled as though a
human signed it. LLM consensus cannot grant institutional approval, establish
source rights or admit a case into the unchanged confirmatory corpus.

The successor protocol and results must retain planned, attempted, valid,
judged and resolved denominators, as well as source exposure, raw votes, missing
outcomes and negative findings. Independent human calibration and broader
project-level sampling remain requirements for stronger scientific claims.
