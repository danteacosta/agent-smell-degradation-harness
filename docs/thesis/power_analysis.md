# Confirmatory Power and Precision Analysis

The independent H1 unit is the source-intent cluster; replications do not add independent degrees of freedom. `protocol.power.estimate_sign_flip_power` uses exact sign enumeration only through 16 clusters and switches to a seeded, add-one-corrected Monte Carlo randomization test for larger designs. This removes the previous `2^n` scalability failure while preserving exact inference for small pilots.

H2 is governed by precision of the held-out, project-clustered `ΔPR-AUC(B3−B0)`, not by the 24-intent pilot floor. `protocol.power.simulate_h2_precision` generates outcome-blind clustered score scenarios and reports median/p90 confidence-interval width, probability that the lower 95% limit clears the practical margin 0.05, and degenerate-bootstrap frequency. No observed labels, outcomes, or fitted parameters may be supplied.

The checked-in candidate evaluates 100 intents across 12 projects under prevalence 0.50, baseline standardized separation 0.45, and provenance increment 0.70. With 200 simulations and 500 project-bootstrap draws (seed 23), it produced estimated margin power 0.875, median CI width 0.1152, p90 width 0.1583, and zero degenerate draws. These values justify a candidate—not a freeze—because the assumed signal is uncertain. Before collection, run a sensitivity grid over prevalence and smaller provenance increments, select the smallest design in the planned 60–100 intent / 8–12 project range that satisfies the frozen thresholds, increase the range if none does, and mark exactly one plan `frozen`.

The confirmatory gate requires: a frozen `h2-precision-plan/v1`; at least 60 intents and 8 projects regardless of simulation; at least 4 intents per project; at least 2 projects per train/calibration/test partition; and at least 12 held-out test intents. A 24-intent/6-project run is reported as pilot only. Falling below the frozen design makes all H2 results descriptive.
