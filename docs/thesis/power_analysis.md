# Confirmatory Power and Precision Analysis

The independent H1 unit is the source-intent cluster; replications do not add independent degrees of freedom. `protocol.power.estimate_sign_flip_power` uses exact sign enumeration only through 16 clusters and switches to a seeded, add-one-corrected Monte Carlo randomization test for larger designs. This removes the previous `2^n` scalability failure while preserving exact inference for small pilots.

H2 is governed by precision of the held-out, project-clustered `ΔPR-AUC(B3−B0)`, not by the 24-intent pilot floor. `protocol.power.simulate_h2_precision` now reproduces the 50/20/30 train/calibration/test project allocation and computes precision only on projects assigned to the untouched test partition. It reports median/p90 confidence-interval width, the probability of satisfying the registered claim rule (`observed ΔPR-AUC ≥ 0.05` and the 95% interval excluding zero), and degenerate-bootstrap frequency. No observed labels, outcomes, or fitted parameters may be supplied.

The corrected conservative candidate evaluates 220 intents across 36 projects with 50/20/30 project allocation, yielding 18/7/11 project quotas and approximately 67 test intents. Under prevalence 0.50, baseline standardized separation 0.45, and a moderate provenance increment 0.50, 200 simulations with 500 project-bootstrap draws (seed 23) produced estimated margin power 0.86, median CI width 0.1344, p90 width 0.1865, and zero degenerate draws. The checked-in sensitivity artifact shows power 0.495 for increment 0.35 and 0.985 for increment 0.70. The weak-signal failure is material: these values justify only a design candidate, never a freeze or a claim that 220/36 is already necessary. The 120-episode pre-pilot and 24-intent/6-project pilot must supply outcome-blind prevalence and variance inputs for the final grid.

The confirmatory gate requires a frozen `h2-precision-plan/v2` whose simulation is explicitly `test_partition_only` and resamples `project_id`; at least 60 intents and 12 projects regardless of simulation; at least 4 intents per project; at least 2 projects per train/calibration partition; and at least 6 projects/24 intents in test. The frozen plan may impose larger minima, as the current candidate does. A 24-intent/6-project run is pilot-only, and any dataset below the frozen design makes H2 descriptive.
